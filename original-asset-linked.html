Shortcuts & tips


Charts
Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, double-click to jump to the latest bar.
Press T trend line, H horizontal line, V vertical line, R rectangle, F Fibonacci, X text, P polyline, A arrow.
Esc cancels, Delete removes the selected drawing, Enter finishes a polyline.


Analysis
Replay steps through history bar by bar. Alerts notify you when price crosses a level. Magnet snaps drawings to open, high, low and close. Save chart image exports a PNG.


Terminal
Press Ctrl+Enter to run. Use Add to chart to keep a script on your charts. Type help() in a script for the full function list.


Search
Press / to search any pair. With live data on, search covers the provider's full ticker list.
